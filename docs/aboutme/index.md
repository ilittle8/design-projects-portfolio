#### What it means to defend an engineering decision
Defending an engineering decision is identifying points of contention, engaging in review, and questioning of what decision were made and why, and explaining your process. It is integral that the engineer in defense discusses with honesty, transparency, and humility. I currently defend my decisions by first clarifying my understanding of what part of my process is being challenged before presenting my relevant calculations, research, and logs. When presenting my work, I am sure to let those who question my choices know why my choices were made using governing equations, theoretical background, and meta analysis of myself as an engineer. Being wrong **_is_** possible, and I make sure to step into the conversation having accepted that possibility.
