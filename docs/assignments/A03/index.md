## Analyze
For my assignment, I was supposed to design a bar with a direct applied load of $300lb_f < F < 500lb_f$. The maximum axial deflection for this load was set to 0.009 inches and the bar was to be made from Aluminium that could be anywhere in the range of $(8.5 - 11.5) \times 10^6\ psi$. I chose the values of 500 lbs of force and 11.5*10^6 psi as young's modulus.

I then was tasked with deciding the values for the cross sectional area of the bar. I chose a 2 inch diameter for the bar. Using this value, I then found the value for the area to be about 3.14 inches squared.

After deciding on the cross sectional area of the bar, I was then able to use the direct tension elongation equation to determine the length of the bar. I found its length to be about 650 inches.
